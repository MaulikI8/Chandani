MADE IN KATHMANDU

The Workshop & The Loom

We do not import flat-packed compromises. Every Chandani piece is shaped by master joiners and weavers working in the Kathmandu Valley.

01 / TIMBER JOINERY

Patan Master Joinery

Located in Lalitpur, our woodworking atelier uses traditional mortise-and-tenon construction. We season native Solid Sisau, American Walnut, and White Oak specifically for Himalayan relative humidity fluctuations.

  • Eased bullnose edges hand-sanded to 400-grit smoothness
  • Organic cold-pressed linseed and botanical wax oils
  • Hidden corner splines ensuring lifetime frame stability
02 / TEXTILE FOUNDATION

Hand-Knotted Tibetan Looms

Woven on heavy vertical wooden looms using high-altitude sheep wool rich in natural protective lanolin. Each knot is individually looped by hand around steel rods and cut with precision shears.

  • 80-knot density providing underfoot cushioning & acoustic absorption
  • Organic mineral and vegetable dye washes
  • Double-warp unbleached organic cotton structural backing
